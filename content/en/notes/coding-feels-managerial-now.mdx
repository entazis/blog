Coding no longer feels like hacking. It feels like managing multiple senior developers in parallel.

My maker's schedule has fragmented into a manager's schedule. Instead of a single deep focus, I'm juggling contexts: asking questions, iterating on approaches, comparing solutions, reviewing outcomes, and looping again.

I spend most of my time defining problems and shaping direction. Once something is clear, I ask for a plan, review it, and if it's LGTM, I let it run. While one agent is implementing, I'm already specifying the next task for another.

The work isn't typing anymore. It's judgment: asking the right questions, spotting weak thinking, and deciding when to move on.
